My name is Shaoqing Zhang, and I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Harbin Institute of Technology. My research focuses on graphical user interface agents (GUI agents), exploring how they can plan effectively, execute actions in dynamic interfaces, and adapt their strategies based on environmental feedback.

I focus on efficient on-device inference for GUI automation, studying device–cloud collaboration and the pruning and deployment of mixture-of-experts (MoE) models to improve inference efficiency on resource-constrained devices. I also explore graph-based representations of the relationships between interface states and actions for agent capability evaluation and failure diagnosis.

First-author

GUITAR: Structured Failure Diagnosis of GUI Agents via State Transitions

Shaoqing Zhang, Kehai Chen, Xuefeng Bai, Zhuosheng Zhang, Pengfei Zhang, Yang Xiang, Min Zhang

NeurIPS 2026 · Evaluations & Datasets Track

arXivPDFCode

TL;DR: GUITAR groups functionally equivalent screens into a state transition graph to diagnose GUI-agent failures. Across the evaluated tasks, 60.4% of failures concentrate in 20% of states.

Instead of treating every screenshot independently, GUITAR analyzes shared functional states and the transitions between them. This exposes recurring bottlenecks and supports targeted guidance to improve agent reliability.

Look Before You Leap: Enhancing Attention and Vigilance Regarding Harmful Content with GuidelineLLM

Shaoqing Zhang, Zhuosheng Zhang, Kehai Chen, Rongxiang Weng, Muyun Yang, Tiejun Zhao, Min Zhang

Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 2025

arXivProceedingsCode

TL;DR: GuidelineLLM separates risk recognition from response generation. A dedicated model produces guidelines that help a responding LLM recognize potentially harmful requests. Only the guideline model needs fine-tuning, making the approach applicable across different responding models.

Dynamic Planning for LLM-based Graphical User Interface Automation

Shaoqing Zhang, Zhuosheng Zhang, Kehai Chen, Xinbei Ma, Muyun Yang, Tiejun Zhao, Min Zhang

Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2024

ACL AnthologyPDFCode

TL;DR: Dynamic Planning of Thoughts (D-PoT) revises plans using the current GUI environment and the agent’s execution history. This supports action prediction in changing interfaces and addresses limitations of relying on lengthy historical dialogues.
